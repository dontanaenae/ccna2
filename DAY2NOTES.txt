HOW TO SUBNET: 2 METHODS
Method 1: 7 to 20 mins: World Solution 
Method 2: 3 Secs: Rivan Finger Method

CSI = Convert, Subtract, IpasokSaHiwa
     Convert: Convert it to bits (figure out the bit length)
    Subtract: Subtract to /32 (32 is the max bits)
IpasokSaHiwa: Ipasok sa hiwa (ipasok sa octet of the original network)

Get the:
Yours (Network address[Family])
First Usable IP (Panganay)
Last Usable IP (Bunso)
Not Yours: Add it again to the network address (doblehin, ipasok ulit sa hiwa)
