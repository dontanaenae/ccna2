### 🎯 Exercise: Compress IPv6 addresses

` 000a:000c:0000:0000:0000:0000:0000:0000/64 `
  = a:c::/64
` 0000:0000:0000:0000:0000:0000:0000:0000/0 `
  = ::/0   = Default Gateway
` fe80:0000:0000:0000:000a:0000:0000:000f/64 `
  = fe80::a:0:0:f/64      = Link-Local
` 2002:6500:0000:3000:0000:0000:0000:0000/64 `
  = 2002:6500:0:3000::/64
` 0000:0000:0000:0000:0000:0000:0000:0001/128 `
  = ::1/128 = Loopback
` ff00:0000:0000:beef:a00a:0aa0:0000:0000/8 `
  = ff00::beef:a00a:aa0:0:0/8 = Multicast Address
